# Mobile Navbar Design

## Goal

Create a compact, balanced mobile header that keeps the full clinic identity, booking access, and menu control visible without crowding.

## Mobile header

- Apply the redesign below 1024px while keeping desktop navigation unchanged.
- Keep the frosted white navigation surface and 60px height.
- Show the clinic logo at approximately 32px with the full `clinic.nameShort` wordmark on one line.
- Replace the wide mobile Book appointment control with a 40px icon-only calendar button carrying an accessible label.
- Show a separate 40px hamburger button.
- Hide the standalone Staff login icon from the mobile header and keep Staff login in the menu.
- Maintain at least 40–44px touch targets.

## Mobile menu

- Open a clean panel directly beneath the fixed header.
- Keep Treatments, Dermatology, Results, Contact, Ask a question, and Location as clear navigation rows.
- Show the current section with the teal accent.
- Keep full-width Book appointment, Staff login, and Call actions in the panel.
- Preserve Escape-to-close, body scroll locking, and focus restoration.

## Verification

- Verify widths of 320px, 360px, 390px, and 430px.
- Confirm the full clinic name, booking icon, and menu icon fit without overlap.
- Confirm menu open/close, Escape, and all navigation targets work.
- Confirm the desktop navbar remains unchanged.
