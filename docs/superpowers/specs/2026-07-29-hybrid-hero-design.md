# Hybrid Hero Design

## Goal

Restore the visual clarity of the previous hero while retaining the current site's accurate clinic information, accessibility, conversion paths, and responsive behavior.

## Desktop layout

- Keep the clinic photograph full-bleed and visually dominant.
- Align the hero content near the bottom of the image rather than vertically centering it.
- Place one compact frosted content card at the bottom-left.
- Place one compact combined location and opening-hours card at the bottom-right.
- Use only a subtle left-and-bottom gradient to protect text contrast. Do not cover the full image with a pale wash.

## Content card

- Show the clinic name as the brand cue.
- Keep the board-certified dermatology badge.
- Keep the headline “Expert care for skin & hair.”
- Keep the concise Pokhara-focused supporting copy.
- Retain the primary Book appointment action and secondary View treatments action.
- Use restrained padding, typography, blur, and shadow so the card does not dominate the photograph.

## Information card

- Keep location and opening hours in one card.
- Separate the two rows with a subtle divider.
- Preserve the verified address, landmark, daily hours, and Saturday OPD note from centralized clinic data.

## Mobile and tablet

- Stack the content card and information card near the bottom.
- Keep both cards compact and readable.
- Preserve the existing 42–44% horizontal image framing so the clinic sign remains visible.
- Keep both CTAs touch-friendly and prevent overflow on narrow screens.

## Accessibility and behavior

- Preserve the meaningful hero image alternative text.
- Preserve reduced-motion-aware scrolling and entry animation behavior.
- Keep the existing `#contact` and `#services` CTA targets.
- Maintain sufficient text contrast against the photograph.

## Verification

- Run the production build.
- Verify desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts in a real browser.
- Confirm that the clinic sign remains visible and neither card obscures the main subject.
- Confirm both hero CTAs still scroll to their intended sections.
