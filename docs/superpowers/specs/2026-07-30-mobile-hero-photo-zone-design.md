# Mobile Hero Photo Zone Design

## Goal

Keep the receptionist and clinic sign clearly visible on phone screens while preserving the hybrid hero content and conversion actions.

## Mobile composition

- Apply the redesign only below 640px.
- Give the hero image a dedicated upper photo zone approximately 30rem tall.
- Frame the image around the clinic sign and receptionist.
- Keep the photo free of cards through the receptionist's focal area.
- Let the content card overlap only the lower edge of the photo.
- Place the combined location and hours card below the content card.
- Use a soft surface background beneath the photo so the stacked cards remain readable.

## Content and behavior

- Preserve all current copy, clinic data, actions, accessibility labels, animations, and scroll targets.
- Keep both action buttons full-width and touch-friendly.
- Use compact spacing and type on mobile without reducing readability.
- Keep the desktop and tablet hybrid layout unchanged.

## Verification

- Verify at 360px, 390px, and 430px widths.
- Confirm the receptionist's face and hands are not covered.
- Confirm the clinic sign remains legible in the image.
- Confirm cards do not overflow and both actions still work.
- Confirm the desktop hero remains unchanged.
