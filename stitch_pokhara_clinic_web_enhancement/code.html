About the clinic

Pokhara Skin and Hair Clinic is located in Prithvi Chowk area, Nayabazar-8, Pokhara — Opposite GMC Hospital. We serve patients across Pokhara and the wider Gandaki region with advanced skin care, hair restoration, and aesthetic dermatology.
